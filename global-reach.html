Through our regional corporate hubs and the AICROS Alliance, we provide sponsors with seamless global trial operations. Combining centralized project governance with native on-the-ground clinical teams across 35+ countries.

The AICROS Collaborative Model

Global Sponsor Oversight • Responsive Local Execution

Conducting multi-regional trials often forces sponsors to choose between monolithic CROs with inflated overheads or small local vendors with fragmented oversight.

VitaCliniq solves this problem through our membership in the Association of International Contract Research Organizations (AICROS). Sponsors work with a single dedicated global study manager while enjoying native clinical research associates (CRAs) who understand local hospital ethics, investigator incentives, and regional health ministries.
