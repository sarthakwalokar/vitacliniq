Bridging Scientific Discovery and Global Patient Access

VitaCliniq Global Research is an accredited, mid-tier clinical research organization designed to offer pharma and biotech innovators the ideal balance: large-scale multi-country reach paired with agile, responsive executive engagement.

Our Founding Purpose

Navigating Regulatory Complexities with Scientific Rigor

VitaCliniq was founded by veteran medical scientists, regulatory directors, and clinical operation executives who recognized that emerging biotech and pharma innovators were frequently underserved by massive, slow-moving contract research organizations.

Our mission is clear: to guide life sciences innovators through complex regulatory environments (including CDSCO, US-FDA, and European Medicines Agency), overcome funding and enrollment constraints, and deliver reliable clinical data on time and within budget.

Responsiveness

RESPONSIVE

Clinical trials face fluid real-world obstacles: recruitment lags, protocol amendments, and evolving safety queries. Our nimble team acts within hours, not weeks.

Rapid IRB/Ethics dossier generation in 6–8 weeks
Dynamic risk-based monitoring with remote CRA check-ins
24/7 dedicated Medical Monitor support for site investigators
Reliability

RELIABLE

A drug or device trial is only as strong as its regulatory auditability. We enforce rigorous quality systems that meet or exceed FDA, EMA, and CDSCO standards.

Continuous eTMF contemporaneous document filing

Full pharmacovigilance oversight with EU-QPPV backup
